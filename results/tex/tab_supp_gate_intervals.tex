\begin{flushleft}\footnotesize
The contrasts of Table~\ref{M-tab:gateinference} with every interval computed. $\delta$ is Cliff's delta of arm A against arm B (A the changed, new or first-named arm), computed per function over the 30 runs of each arm and averaged over the functions; negative values favour A. Inference is conditional on the 29 CEC2017 functions treated as exchangeable instances of the class the suite represents. Percentile and BCa intervals are read from the same 20{,}000 bootstrap resamples of the functions (seed 20260913, drawn in the order of \texttt{analyze\_gate\_inference.py}, so the percentile intervals are the ones it reports); the Student-$t$ interval uses $t_{28}$ and is truncated to $[-1,1]$. The adjusted intervals are at $1-0.05/4$, the Bonferroni level for each family of four contrasts within which a claim is decided. $p$ is the unadjusted two-sided signed-rank test of the 29 per-function deltas (its Holm-adjusted value is in Table~\ref{M-tab:gateinference}). Verdicts as in Table~\ref{M-tab:gateinference}; the penultimate column gives the BCa verdicts, followed by the Student-$t$ ones where they differ, and the last column the verdicts of the Bonferroni-adjusted Student-$t$ interval. Where the last differ from the unadjusted verdicts, the verdict does not survive the family adjustment, and Table~\ref{M-tab:gateinference} marks it with $^{\dagger}$. The interval of the budget interaction with L\'evy off excludes zero with a bound within 0.02 of it, and Table~\ref{M-tab:gateinference} reports it as a tie (main text, Section~2.6).
