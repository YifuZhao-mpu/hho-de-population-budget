CEC2017 is the diagnostic suite. The CEC2014$^{\dagger}$ columns are \emph{exploratory}: these comparisons are not among those the pre-registration lists, they were run after the confirmatory analysis, and the pre-registration requires any such comparison to be labelled exploratory. Rows 1 and 2 each change one constant, $N_{\text{init}}$, inside one algorithm; the L-SHADE arms keep every other setting as in our port of the published algorithm ($H=6$, $|\mathcal{A}|=2.6N$, $p=0.11$, bound handling, memory update). Rows 3 and 4 compare two different algorithms: besides $N_{\text{init}}$ (row 4 only), SEHHO-COBL-R and L-SHADE differ in $H$, archive rate and $p$, and in implementation details (bound handling, steady-state versus generational replacement, the CR memory mean, L-SHADE's terminal CR value, $F$ sampling, rounding of the pbest pool). The L-SHADE $18D$ arm of rows 1 and 4 is an independent re-run of stock L-SHADE with its own seed stream, not the L-SHADE runs of Tables~\ref{S-tab:cec2014}, \ref{S-tab:cec2017} and~\ref{S-tab:cec2017_tight}, so row 4 differs from the matching contrasts there by sampling error (at most $0.024$ in $\delta$). Row 1 is what licenses a statement about population sizing; row 3 is what licenses a statement about SEHHO-COBL-R. They point in different directions, and both are reported.
