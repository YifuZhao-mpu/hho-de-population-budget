Friedman average rank within each block (lower is better; position of 7 in parentheses), over the 12 CEC2022 functions with 30 runs per cell; bold marks the rule with the best mean rank, which is the one selected. All candidates are gate-free and differ only in how the population is sized. This is the only constant fitted in the study, and it is fitted on CEC2022, the selection suite. LPSR-6D ranked first in both tight blocks and 5th and 3rd of 7 at the large budgets, where LPSR-18D ranked first: averaging ranks over blocks does not prevent a rule from winning through the tight budgets, and the selected $N_{\text{init}}=6D$ is fixed at every budget, so it does not size the population to the budget. N120b is a control: the N120 rule with the schedule basis changed to match what LPSR requires.
